Rescues operations underway in New Orleans after tanker hits river cruise boat

Society📍 New Orleans, United States 🇺🇸 · 2026-11-13 · 📰 Reported by 2 outlets

📌 What happened

An asphalt tanker collided with a river cruise boat on the Mississippi River in New Orleans, causing the boat to partially submerge and flip. Dozens of passengers were hospitalized, but all were accounted for with no critical injuries. Rescue operations involving multiple agencies are ongoing.

📖 The full story

The Atlantic Asphalt tanker struck the American Heritage paddleboat on Friday at about 12:30pm local time (18:30 GMT) in New Orleans. The cruise boat had docked two hours earlier to disembark passengers when the collision occurred, causing it to sink and topple over. Mayor Helena Moreno stated that all 129 passengers were accounted for, with 32 taken to local hospitals, none with critical injuries.

Passengers were seen entering the water as the boat listed, with water temperature at 19 degrees Celsius (66 degrees Fahrenheit). More than a dozen people in life jackets were rescued from the river onto nearby ships. Witness Jamie Holford described the event as 'surreal,' noting it took 15 minutes from impact to the boat rolling over.

Port police, the fire department, the coastguard, and emergency medical staff responded to the scene, setting up a triage area in the port's administration building. The river was closed to vessel traffic. American Cruise Lines, which operates the boat, confirmed the evacuation of guests and crew, expressing concern for those involved. The American Heritage, built in 2015, holds up to 150 passengers and was on an eight-day cruise. The 160-metre tanker, flagged in the Marshall Islands, specializes in asphalt and bitumen, but the cause of the collision and whether it carried cargo remains unknown.

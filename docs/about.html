About Wire Now

Wire Now is a free news app for everyone in Sri Lanka: Sri Lankan news first, then the world. Our idea is simple: give you the key information of every story first (what happened, why it matters, the key facts and what comes next), then the full details if you want them, in English, Sinhala or Tamil.

How it works

1. We follow the news. Every 15 minutes, Wire Now checks Sri Lankan and international news outlets. Stories reported by more outlets come first.

2. We read across sources. For each story we read several reports of the same event. Where outlets disagree, we show both versions and who said what.

3. We summarise and check. An AI writes the summary using only facts from those reports. Every number and name is checked against the originals, and a second AI from a different company checks every claim. A summary that fails is not published.

4. We always link the source. Every story links to the original report.

5. We remember. Facts from every checked story go into the Wire Now Fact Base, with who reported them and when. It powers "Is this true?", the story so far, and our explanations.

Editorial standards

Accuracy first. Only facts from published reports. No guesses, no invented details. Numbers and names must match the source exactly.

Fairness. We do not take sides. When reports disagree, both versions are shown.

Confirmed means confirmed. A fact is marked confirmed only when two or more independent outlets report it.

"Is this true?" answers only with evidence from reliable reports and fact-checkers. With no reliable source, the answer is "can't verify", never a guess.

Every language, properly. Translations must be complete and correct, or the story waits until they are.

No drawn faces. We never make up pictures of real people.

Corrections

When we get something wrong, we fix it and learn from it: every mistake becomes an automatic check so it cannot happen again. Tell us about a mistake through our Telegram bot.

What we are not

Wire Now does not do its own reporting yet. Our summaries are only as accurate as the reports they come from, so for important decisions, please read the original article.

"இது உண்மையா?" நம்பகமான அறிக்கைகள் மற்றும் உண்மை சரிபார்ப்பாளர்களின் ஆதாரங்களைக் கொண்டு மட்டுமே பதிலளிக்கிறது. நம்பகமான ஆதாரம் இல்லையெனில் பதில் "உறுதிப்படுத்த முடியவில்லை".

ஒவ்வொரு மொழியிலும் சரியாக. மொழிபெயர்ப்புகள் முழுமையாகவும் சரியாகவும் இருக்க வேண்டும்; இல்லையெனில் அவை தயாராகும் வரை செய்தி காத்திருக்கும்.

வரைந்த முகங்கள் இல்லை. உண்மையான மனிதர்களின் படங்களை நாங்கள் ஒருபோதும் உருவாக்குவதில்லை.

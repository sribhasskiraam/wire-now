About Wire Now

Wire Now is a free news service for people in Sri Lanka. It brings you Sri Lankan news first, then the world, explained in short cards you can read in under a minute.

How it works

1. We follow the news. Every 15 minutes, Wire Now checks Sri Lankan and international news outlets, and puts the stories reported by the most outlets first.

2. We read across sources. For big stories, we read several reports of the same event. Where outlets disagree, for example on a number of people injured, we show both.

3. We summarise and fact-check. An AI writes the summary using only facts from those reports. Every number and name is then checked automatically against the original articles, and a second AI checks every claim. A summary that fails is not published.

4. We always link the source. Every story links to the original report, so you can read the full article.

What we are not

Wire Now does not do its own reporting or take sides. Our summaries are only as accurate as the reports they come from, so for important decisions, please read the original article.

📖 The full story

According to Newswire, Sri Lanka joined 150 other countries in supporting a United Nations resolution that affirms the permanent sovereignty of the Palestinian people over their natural resources in the occupied Palestinian territories, including East Jerusalem. The United Nations General Assembly’s Second Committee approved the resolution on November 11, 2026. The vote was 151 countries in favour, seven against, and 11 abstaining.

The seven countries that voted against were the United States, Israel, Colombia, Micronesia, Nauru, Palau and Paraguay. Sri Lanka voted in favour alongside countries including India, China and the United Kingdom. The resolution is titled Permanent sovereignty of the Palestinian people in the Occupied Palestinian Territory, including East Jerusalem, and of the Arab population in the occupied Syrian Golan over their natural resources.

It reaffirms that people living under occupation have the right to control and benefit from their natural resources. It addresses concerns over the exploitation, damage and depletion of natural resources, including land and water resources, in the occupied territories. The text calls on Israel, as the occupying power, to stop activities that exploit or destroy Palestinian natural resources, and recognises the Palestinians’ right to seek compensation for losses caused by such activities.

The vote shows broad international support for Palestinian economic rights and sovereignty amid continuing concerns over conditions in the occupied territories. Sri Lanka has maintained longstanding support for Palestinian self-determination and a negotiated two-state solution to the Israeli-Palestinian conflict. The resolution was approved at the committee stage and will proceed to the full UN General Assembly for final consideration, though it is not legally binding and carries political significance as an expression of UN member states’ positions.
